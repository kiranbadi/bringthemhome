favorite_border Our Mission

My mission with MissingLives is to make it easier to gather, discover, broadcast, and share information about missing persons. We want to bring families, communities, and law enforcement together, using technology to improve access to information, encourage community contributions, and help turn credible leads into action. Our goal is not to replace existing missing-person databases, but to complement them by increasing awareness, improving collaboration, and ultimately helping locate missing individuals and reunite them with their families and loved ones.
